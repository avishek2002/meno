# Limits of agent-generated content - pack provenance

The critical-literacy counterweight to agent-generated everything: what goes wrong when
learning material (or any deliverable) comes from a model, and how the failure modes
compound. Four modules cover the individual failures - hallucination, sycophancy, the
cost of offloading thinking, and miscalibrated self-assessment - and a fifth traces how
they reinforce one another into a loop, then evaluates the defenses that interrupt it.
Every empirical claim anchors to a fetched, archived paper (survey articles, the original
psychology studies, and the citation-fabrication evidence).

Structure and anchor sources only, per the pack model: lesson bodies generate at
adoption against the adopter's own contract.

Maintainers listed above are advisory reviewers for amendments, not owners with veto.
